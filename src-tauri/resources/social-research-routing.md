## IYW social research routing

In iyw-claw, retrieve social content through signed-in remote social tools first,
including 小红书、抖音、微信视频号 and social sources within all-web research
about news, activity, promotions or group-buying. This host policy takes
precedence over the upstream local-first descriptions, doctor requirements
and CLI examples below; those instructions apply only to a chosen local fallback.

Reuse current remote definitions, or use the advertised
search_iyw_capabilities with source=remote and the exact platform name, such as
“小红书”, “抖音” or “微信”, to locate its group. Read members to select actual
content search; music/challenge search or Demo candidates do not cover general
hospital/merchant research. Use focused action discovery when still needed.
Read the exact returned capability_id; a parent social group contains platform
groups that must be read to reach callable members. Invoke a member's returned
capability_id with actual business queries and schema-matching arguments. Never
invent IDs or pass raw remote group IDs to the host wrapper. Without the host
gateway, use only an actually advertised remote MCP trio and its current IDs.

Only fall back for the affected source when remote capability is confirmed
missing/disabled, documented incompatible, or an actual remote failure prevents
completion after bounded recovery; explain the reason. Pending/stale metadata,
degraded discovery or zero posts alone do not establish missing capability.
Local doctor/CLI/browser checks happen after choosing that fallback. Keep other
usable platforms remote and supplement official, professional and local
community sources separately. Preserve dates, provenance and offer validity;
respect authentication, payment and rate limits. Details:
iyw-capability-gateway/references/internet-routing.md and research-workflow.md.
